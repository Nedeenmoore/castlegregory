Good to know

Admission is free

All three sessions are free to attend, with donations warmly welcomed. Concerts begin at 7:30pm. Arrive a little early, as seating is informal and fills quickly. The simplest way to come is the bus. Local Link route 273 runs from Tralee through Castlegregory to Cloghane and Brandon, and it saves you the lanes in the dark: timetables and the rest of it. If you drive, there is no formal car park at Cloghane. Come early and be ready to share: directions and parking.

Register to learn of pop-up performances in the pubs and in spectacular natural settings on Dingle’s North Shore, in arrangements by Quatuor Ébène, the Danish String Quartet, Vision String Quartet and Philharmonix.
