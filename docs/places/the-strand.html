Sand

The beaches

There are two beaches here and they are easy to confuse, so it is worth being clear. Castlegregory Beach is the strand at the end of Strand Street, about 1.1km from the village centre, a walk of fifteen minutes, with a car park near the end of the road. Magherabeg, which you will also see written Maharabeg, Maherabeg and Béal Geal, and which the signs sometimes call Sandy Bay, is three and a half kilometres north of the village and is the developed one.

Both of these face east, into the sheltered water of Tralee Bay. The long exposed Atlantic strand that people mean when they say Brandon Bay is on the other side of the spit, at Stradbally, Gowlane and Kilcummin and on towards Fermoyle and Cloghane. That side is for surf. This side is for swimming.

Magherabeg is the one with everything: it holds the Blue Flag, it has the lifeguards in season, there is parking and there are watersports operators working off the sand. Castlegregory Beach is quieter and holds a Green Coast Award instead, which recognises water quality and natural beauty at a beach with fewer facilities.

The facts

Castlegregory Beach
End of Strand Street, 1.1km from the village. Green Coast Award. No lifeguards.
Magherabeg
3.4km north, six minutes by car. Blue Flag since 2010, lifeguarded in season.
Lifeguards
Start of June to mid-September, noon to 7pm weekdays and 11am to 7pm at weekends and bank holidays
Water quality
Both classified Excellent in the 2025 bathing season results
Facilities
Portable toilets at Magherabeg, free from March to October, with picnic tables and recycling bins
Parking
Informal, on the grass, and it fills up on a fine day

Good to know

Lifeguard cover is weekends only through June, then every day in July and August, and the exact times are posted on the noticeboard at Magherabeg. There are no lifeguards at Castlegregory Beach at all, and no official confirmation that it is a safe place to swim.

The whole beach and dune system is a Special Area of Conservation and the dunes are actively eroding. Use the marked accesses and keep off the dune faces. Dogs must be on a lead at Magherabeg and owners must clean up.

The roads out to both beaches are narrow.
