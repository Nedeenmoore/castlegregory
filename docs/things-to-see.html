Things to do in Castlegregory and the Maharees

What follows is the short list of what is genuinely worth the drive, arranged by where it is. Nothing here is more than half an hour from the village.

For the water itself, surfing, windsurfing, diving, paddling and the two saunas, see the water sports page. For walks of any length, from a stroll on the strand to Mount Brandon, see getting around.

Shopping

Two practical warnings first. Cloghane and Brandon have no petrol station, no cash machine and no pharmacy: the nearest of each is in Castlegregory. And if you are self-catering, do the big shop in Tralee or Dingle on the way in, because the village shops are for what you forgot rather than for a week's groceries.

Things to do in Dingle

The Harry Clarke Windows Six double-lancet windows by Ireland's greatest stained-glass artist, installed in March 1924 in the convent chapel on the grounds of St Mary's Church, Green Street. Scenes from the life of Christ, in the jewelled colour and strange elongated faces that made Clarke's name. The chapel is now cared for by An Díseart; worth the drive over the pass. One request

The dune system on the Maharees is the reason this coast looks the way it does, and it has retreated more than forty metres in recent decades. The Maharees Conservation Association has planted marram and built two kilometres of chestnut fencing to hold it together. Use the marked beach accesses, keep off the dunes, do not drive on them, camp only in the official sites and take your litter home.
