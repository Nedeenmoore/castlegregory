Between concerts

Things to see and do

The concerts are in the evening, which leaves the whole of the day. What follows is the short list of what is genuinely worth the drive, arranged by where it is. Nothing here is more than half an hour from either venue.

For the water itself, surfing, windsurfing, diving, paddling and the two saunas, see the water sports page. For walks of any length, from a stroll on the strand to Mount Brandon, see getting around.

The list

Castlegregory

The Strand Miles of golden sand along Brandon Bay, one of the longest beaches in Ireland, minutes from the village. Discover Ireland ↗ · Map it ↗
Lough Gill A gentle lakeside loop just behind the dunes, famous for its swans, birdlife and rare natterjack toads. About the lake ↗ · Map it ↗
St. Mary's Church The village church at the heart of Castlegregory, a quiet place to pause. Sunday Mass at 11.30am. Parish ↗ · Map it ↗
Castlegregory Golf Club Nine-hole links squeezed between Lough Gill and the Atlantic: golf with dunes, sea and mountains for company. Official site ↗ · Map it ↗
Glanteenassig Forest Park A hidden valley of forest, lakes and boardwalk trails in the Slieve Mish mountains, ten minutes from the village. Coillte ↗ · Map it ↗
Maharees Heritage Trail A self-guided walk out around the tip of the peninsula, with panels along the way on the dunes, the islands and the fishing life. Free pocket maps from the information centre on Tailor's Row. The route ↗ · Map it ↗

Brandon / Cloghane

Brandon Point Dramatic sea cliffs and panoramic views over Brandon Bay and the Maharees, one of the best places in Kerry to watch the sunset. Discover Ireland ↗ · Map it ↗
Brandon Bay A fantastic spot for swimming, surfing, fishing, and simply enjoying the scenery, with Murphy's pub keeping watch from the pier. Dingle Peninsula ↗ · Map it ↗
Brandon Bay Boat Hire Self-drive boats out of Brandon for a few hours' fishing on the bay, with rods, tackle and bait to hire and lifejackets aboard. Boats & rates ↗ · Map it ↗
Grotto of St Brendan A shrine to the seafaring saint who gave the mountain its name. At Faha above Cloghane, where the pilgrim path begins. Its story ↗ · Map it ↗
Climb Mount Brandon Ireland's highest peak outside the Reeks (952m). The Faha route from Cloghane is the classic pilgrim's way up: 9km and about four hours. Route guide ↗ · Map it ↗

Dingle

The Harry Clarke Windows Six double-lancet windows by Ireland's greatest stained-glass artist, installed in March 1924 in the convent chapel on the grounds of St Mary's Church, Green Street. Scenes from the life of Christ, in the jewelled colour and strange elongated faces that made Clarke's name. The chapel is now cared for by An Díseart; worth the drive over the pass. The National Museum on Clarke ↗ · Visit & tours ↗ · Map it ↗

One request

The dune system on the Maharees is the reason this coast looks the way it does, and it has retreated more than forty metres in recent decades. The Maharees Conservation Association has planted marram and built two kilometres of chestnut fencing to hold it together. Use the marked beach accesses, keep off the dunes, do not drive on them, camp only in the official sites and take your litter home.
