Where to eat and drink in Castlegregory

This is a small coast and its kitchens keep small-coast hours. Several places close one or two days a week, some are seasonal, and the good ones fill on a summer evening. Please ring ahead, particularly on a summer evening or when something is on in the village, when a place that normally feeds forty people is feeding rather more.

If you are eating before an evening out, book for six or a little before; if you would rather eat afterwards, check the kitchen's closing time when you ring, because last orders here are earlier than you may be used to.
