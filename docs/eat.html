Food and coffee

Where to eat and drink

This is a small coast and its kitchens keep small-coast hours. Several places close one or two days a week, some are seasonal, and the good ones fill on a summer evening. Please ring ahead, particularly on a concert night, when a village that normally feeds forty people is feeding rather more.

A concert begins at half past seven. If you want to eat first, book for six or a little before; if you would rather eat afterwards, check the kitchen's closing time when you ring, because last orders here are earlier than you may be used to.
